      Definitions and historical coverage

      Claimed and closed discovery tasks are counted once each. One task can include several C scouts; physical probe attempts are not recorded consistently. Usable means a task has audited valid C, unchanged replay and comparison evidence. Qualified mismatches exclude duplicates, setup failures and unattributed runner differences. Later admission is attributed back to the original discovery time; recent samples may still be awaiting qualification. Source surveys and linked validation or correction tasks are excluded from the discovery-task denominator. Unknown evidence classifications remain visible. The local exploration coalescer waits 15 minutes after changed discovery evidence before a checked snapshot publication. Integration or pending CI can delay it. This is not a live feed; the capture timestamp shows actual freshness.
